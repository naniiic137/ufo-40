# The promo's edit: one shot per line, in order.
#   CLIP  FIRST  FRAMES  [EFFECT=VALUE ...]
# CLIP is a recording made by tools/promo/*.ufs or tools/promo/clips/*.ufs
# (build/promo/clips/CLIP/ and CLIP.wav), FIRST its first frame and FRAMES
# how many to use. 60 frames a second; the montage track runs at 150 bpm
# from the first frame, so a beat is 24 frames, a bar 96, and every cut
# lands on a beat (most on a bar line). tools/promo/fx.c draws the effects:
#   in=flash|glitch|mosaic|dither|zoom|cut   how the shot comes in
#   zoom=F,F      zoom punches (x1.08) at these frames of the shot
#   shake=F,F     screen shakes at these frames
#   cap=slide|drop|slam   how the caption (the clip script's overlay_text
#                 lines) comes in; the TRIBUTE TO line types on after it
#   title=TEXT sub=TEXT badge=TEXT   words of its own ('_' is a space)
#   hold=F        the recording stops moving at frame F
#   as=hook|end   the boot, under the hook's or the end card's words
# The big moments sit on beats: FIRST is picked so the hit is at 24 or 72.
# "grid" is the 22-games counter; "tile CLIP FIRST" adds a game that is
# only in the grid.
